Puzzles

Hints that don't ruin the puzzle.

Stuck? Say so. Help comes one step at a time, and each step leaves you something to find.

  1. A question. "What is your knight standing in front of?"
  2. A highlighted piece. The coach points. You look.
  3. The pattern's name. "Think clearance."
  4. The solution. Only when you ask for it.

Play

Play someone who can explain the game.

Pick a strength from 500 to 3000. Ask about the position at any point. Take a move back. When it's over, ask to go through it and the game opens in review.

The coaching thins out as the rating climbs. At 500 you hear about a threat before it lands. At 3000 you get the engine, and very little conversation until it's over.

Review

Don't watch your mistake. Replay it.

Paste a game. The board goes back to the move before each one that cost you. The coach asks what you were thinking. Then you try again.

Any move that holds the position counts. The engine's first choice is one of them, and often there are others.

Memory

Your next lesson starts where the last one ended.

After each session the board writes down what happened: what you solved cleanly, what needed hints, which mistakes came up in your games. The coach reads it before saying hello.

Every session is kept, so the notes add up to a profile: what you're getting better at, and what still catches you.
